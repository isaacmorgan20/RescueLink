RescueLink

Log out of RescueLink?

You’ll be signed out on this device. Your active SOS settings and emergency contacts stay saved to your account.

Before you log out

  • You won’t be able to trigger SOS from this device until you log in again.
  • Any in‑progress alerts will keep running and responders stay updated.
  • You can log back in at any time to regain full access.

Need to secure your account? Use “Sign out of all devices” from Settings next time.
