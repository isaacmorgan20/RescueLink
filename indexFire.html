Send a fire SOS?

We’ll alert nearby fire responders and your emergency contacts, and share your live location.

By sending a fire SOS, RescueLink will:

  • • Notify the nearest fire response center with your current location.
  • • Alert your chosen emergency contacts by SMS and email.
  • • Keep your location updating in real time as you move.

No signal? If you’re offline, your SOS will be queued and sent automatically as soon as your connection returns.
